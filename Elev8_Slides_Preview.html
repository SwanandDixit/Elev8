Elev8 | Skill-Verified Hyperlocal Blue-Collar Employment Platform 1
Why Elev8: empowering skilled workers without friction
The problem
India’s 450M+ blue-collar workforce suffers from informal fragmentation, predatory contractor cuts (up to 30%), unverified skill claims, and zero payment guarantees. Employers face ghosting and opaque pricing. Elev8 answers: how can a mobile-first platform provide instant hyperlocal matching, explainable pricing, bilateral wage negotiation, and guaranteed payment escrow?
Objectives
• Spatial Bounding: Filter workers using exact Haversine distance in O(N)
• Dynamic Price Estimation: Compute fair trade rates with urgency surcharges (+20%, +40%)
• Bilateral Negotiation Engine: Structured state-machine for offer/counter-offer convergence
• Escrow Financial Security: Tokenized 5% booking deposit via Razorpay with dispute protection
• Skill Verification: Transparent credentialing and reputation rating feedback loops
DAA

Orchestration & State Management
The Elev8 core engine coordinates each stage through a reactive Zustand state machine coupled with Supabase PostgreSQL tables. Every transition is atomic: a job cannot enter the "Hired" state until Razorpay confirms cryptographic webhook verification of the 5% deposit.
